
# 🦟 Dengue Data Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Dengue Data Analysis Dashboard built with Microsoft Excel to explore dengue test results by gender, age group, and area type. It also highlights the areas with the highest number of positive cases.

The main goal of this dashboard is to help users understand the number of affected people, the infection rate, and how positive cases are spread across different groups and areas.

## 📊 Executive Overview

### 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Patients | 1,000 |
| Affected People | 533 |
| Infection Rate | 53.3% |
| Average Age (Affected) | 35.924 |

The dashboard shows 1,000 total patient records, with 533 affected people. The overall infection rate displayed on the dashboard is 53.3%.

### 💡 Key Insight

More than half of the patient records have a positive outcome in this dataset.

## 👥 Positive Cases by Gender

The dashboard compares positive cases and infection rates for female and male patients.

| Gender | Total Patients | Affected Patients | Infection Rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Female | 524 | 281 | 53.63% |
| Male | 476 | 252 | 52.94% |

### 💡 Key Insight

Female patients account for 281 positive cases, while male patients account for 252. The infection rates are close, with a slightly higher rate among female patients.

## 👶 Positive Cases by Age Group

The dashboard shows how positive cases are spread across three age groups.

| Age Group | Positive Cases |
|---|---:|
| Adult (18-59) | 386 |
| Child (0-17) | 101 |
| Senior (60+) | 46 |
| **Total** | **533** |

### 💡 Key Insight

Adults have the highest number of positive cases, with 386 cases. Children account for 101 cases, while seniors account for 46.

Adults also make up the largest share of the positive cases shown on the dashboard.

## 🏘️ Positivity Rate by Area Type

The dashboard compares the share of positive cases between developed and undeveloped areas.

| Area Type | Share of Positive Cases |
|---|---:|
| Developed | 48.22% |
| Undeveloped | 51.78% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

### 💡 Key Insight

Undeveloped areas account for 51.78% of all positive cases shown, while developed areas account for 48.22%.

**Note:** These values show each area's share of all positive cases. They do not show the infection rate within each area type.

## 📍 Most Affected Areas

The dashboard highlights five areas with the highest positive case counts among the areas displayed.

| Area | Positive Cases |
|---|---:|
| Jatrabari | 31 |
| Demra | 26 |
| Tejgaon | 24 |
| Bangshal | 24 |
| Mohammadpur | 20 |

### 💡 Key Insight

Jatrabari has the highest number of positive cases among the five areas, with 31 cases. Demra follows with 26 cases.

Tejgaon and Bangshal each have 24 positive cases, while Mohammadpur has 20.

## 🎯 Key Findings

1. 🦟 **Overall Cases:** 533 out of 1,000 patient records are marked as affected.
2. 👥 **Gender:** Female patients have slightly more positive cases and a slightly higher infection rate than male patients.
3. 👶 **Age Group:** Adults account for the highest number of positive cases.
4. 🏘️ **Area Type:** Undeveloped areas account for a slightly larger share of all positive cases.
5. 📍 **Top Area:** Jatrabari has the highest positive case count among the five areas shown.

## ⚠️ Data Note

This dashboard presents results from the available dataset only. The findings should not be treated as the actual dengue rate for the whole city or country.

The area type percentages show the share of positive cases in each area type, not the infection rate among all patients in those areas.
