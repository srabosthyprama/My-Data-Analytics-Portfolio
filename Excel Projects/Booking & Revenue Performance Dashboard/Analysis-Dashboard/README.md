# 🏨 Aurora Suites - Booking & Revenue Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Hotel Booking & Revenue Performance Dashboard** built to help management understand the hotel's revenue performance, booking behavior, cancellation risk, customer value, distribution channels, and demand patterns.

The dashboard tells one main story:

> **Aurora Suites has strong booking demand, but too much revenue is at risk because of cancellations and long booking lead times. The biggest opportunity is to turn existing demand into more reliable and profitable revenue.**

## Executive Overview - What Is Happening?

### Key KPIs

- **Total Revenue:** $29.6M
- **Total Bookings:** 118.7K
- **Average Lead Time:** 104 days
- **Cancellation Rate:** 37.2%
- **Lost Revenue:** -$13.1M

The hotel has a strong business base with **$29.6M in revenue and 118.7K bookings**. However, the **37.2% cancellation rate** is the main concern.

With an average lead time of **104 days**, many guests book far in advance, giving them more time to change their plans. This creates a large gap between **expected revenue and actual revenue**, resulting in approximately **$13.1M in lost revenue**.

### Manager's Takeaway

**The priority should not only be getting more bookings. It should be protecting the bookings the hotel already has.**

---

## ADR by Customer Type - Who Is More Valuable?

The ADR chart compares the average daily rate paid by different customer types.

- **Transient:** $108 — Highest ADR
- **Contract:** $88
- **Transient-Party:** $86
- **Group:** $84

Transient customers generate the highest room value.

### Insight

The hotel should pay attention to **customer mix**, not just booking volume. During high-demand periods, rooms should be prioritized for segments that generate stronger revenue.

### Recommendation

- Focus on attracting high-value transient customers.
- Use dynamic pricing during busy periods.
- Maintain a balanced customer mix while giving more attention to segments that bring higher revenue.

---

## Cancellation Rate by Lead Time - Where Is the Biggest Risk?

The chart shows that:

- **0–30 days:** 16.19%
- **More than 30 days:** 83.81%

The biggest cancellation exposure comes from bookings made **more than 30 days in advance**.

### Insight

Long booking windows give the hotel early visibility, but they also create more time for customers to cancel.

### Recommendation

For long-lead bookings:

- Use deposits for selected high-risk bookings.
- Promote non-refundable rate options.
- Send pre-arrival reminders.
- Use different cancellation policies based on demand and customer risk.

**Goal:** Convert more future bookings into confirmed revenue.

---

## Top 10 Countries by Booking - Where Does Demand Come From?

Portugal is the largest booking market by a wide margin, followed by the UK, France, Spain, and Germany.

### Insight

The hotel has a strong customer base in a few key markets. However, high booking volume does not always mean high profitability.

### Recommendation

- Focus marketing on countries with high bookings and strong revenue.
- Create special offers for valuable markets.
- Reduce spending on markets with low revenue or high cancellations.
  
This will help management identify **high-value markets**, not just high-volume markets.

---

## Lead Time & Revenue by Distribution Channel - High Revenue, but Higher Cancellation Risk

### Insight

- **Online Travel Agents:** $17M revenue + 109-day lead time → High revenue but more time to cancel.
- **Offline Travel Agents:** $7M revenue + 136-day lead time → Higher cancellation risk.
- **Direct:** $4M revenue + 58-day lead time → Lower revenue but shorter booking window.
- **Corporate:** $1M revenue + 45-day lead time → Lowest revenue but lowest cancellation risk.

### Recommendation

Keep using **OTAs** for high revenue, but reduce cancellation risk with clear cancellation policies and deposits for risky bookings. At the same time, **grow direct and corporate bookings** to build more stable revenue.

---

## Monthly Revenue Trend - When Does the Hotel Perform Best?

Revenue increases through the year and reaches its highest point around **August**, followed by a sharp decline.

### Insight

The hotel has a clear seasonal revenue pattern.

### Recommendation

During peak periods:

- Increase room rates.
- Protect premium inventory.
- Focus on high-value customers.
- Use stricter cancellation policies where appropriate.

During weaker periods:

- Use targeted promotions.
- Encourage direct bookings.
- Create attractive packages.
- Target key international markets.

**The pricing strategy should change with demand rather than staying the same throughout the year.**

---

# 🎯 Final Recommendations

### 1. Reduce cancellation risk
Focus on long-lead bookings with deposits, flexible but controlled cancellation policies, and pre-arrival communication.

### 2. Protect high-value demand
Prioritize customer segments with stronger ADR, especially during peak periods.

### 3. Reduce OTA dependency
Keep using OTAs for reach, but build strategies to convert repeat guests into direct customers.

### 4. Use seasonal pricing
Maximize rates during peak demand and use targeted offers during slower periods.

### 5. Measure channel profitability
Look beyond booking volume and compare channels using **revenue, ADR, cancellations, lead time, and commission costs**.
