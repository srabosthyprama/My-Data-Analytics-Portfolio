# ☕ Coffee Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Coffee Sales Performance Dashboard** built to help coffee shop management monitor sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product popularity, and business trends across multiple store locations.

The dashboard answers one central business question:

> **The coffee business is growing steadily, but a small number of products and categories generate most of the revenue. Understanding customer buying patterns, peak hours, and top-performing locations helps maximize sales and improve operational decisions.**

# Executive Overview - What Is Happening?

## Key KPIs

- **Total Revenue:** $699K
- **Total Footfalls:** 149K
- **Average Orders per Customer:** 1.44
- **Average Bill per Customer:** $4.69

The coffee business generated **$699K in revenue** from approximately **149K customer visits**.

On average, each customer places **1.44 orders**, with an average spending of **$4.69 per visit**. While customer traffic is strong, increasing basket size and encouraging additional purchases can further improve revenue.

### Manager's Takeaway

**Customer traffic is healthy. The next opportunity is increasing revenue from every customer visit through upselling and cross-selling.**

---

# Monthly Sales Trend - Is Business Growing?

The monthly sales trend shows consistent business growth throughout the first half of the year.

- February: **$76K**
- March: **$99K**
- April: **$119K**
- May: **$157K**
- June: **$166K** *(Highest)*

### Insight

Sales continue to increase month after month, indicating growing customer demand and improving business performance.

### Recommendation

- Prepare inventory for increasing demand.
- Maintain sufficient staffing during busy months.

- Ethiopia
- Sustainably Grown Organic

---

# Sales by Weekday - Which Days Are Busiest?

Sales remain relatively consistent throughout the week, with slight increases toward the beginning and end of the work week.

Highest sales:

- Monday
- Friday
- Thursday

Lower sales:

- Saturday
- Sunday

### Insight

Weekday demand is stronger than weekend demand, suggesting the business benefits from office workers and daily commuters.

### Recommendation

- Increase staffing during busy weekdays.
- Introduce weekend promotions to improve customer traffic.
- Launch loyalty rewards to encourage repeat weekend visits.

---

# Product Category Performance - Which Categories Drive Revenue?

Coffee dominates total sales by a wide margin.

Top categories include:

- Coffee — **$270K**
- Tea — **$196K**
- Bakery — **$82K**
- Drinking Chocolate — **$72K**

### Insight

Coffee and Tea account for the majority of total revenue, while other categories contribute much less.

### Recommendation

- Continue expanding premium coffee offerings.
- Promote bakery and chocolate items alongside beverages.
- Use combo deals to increase sales of lower-performing categories.

---

# Size Distribution Based on Orders - What Size Do Customers Prefer?

Order size distribution is relatively balanced.

- Regular — **32.73%**
- Large — **31.96%**
- Not Defined — **25.33%**
- Small — **9.99%**

### Insight

Most customers prefer Regular and Large beverages.

The large percentage of **"Not Defined"** sizes also suggests an opportunity to improve data quality.

### Recommendation

- Focus promotions on Regular and Large drinks.
- Standardize order entry to reduce missing size information.
- Encourage upgrades from Regular to Large through small price incentives.

---

# Hourly Sales Trend - When Are Customers Buying?

Sales increase rapidly during the morning hours, peak around **9 AM – 10 AM**, and gradually decline throughout the day.

### Insight

Morning is the busiest sales period, likely driven by commuters purchasing coffee before work.

### Recommendation

- Schedule more staff during morning peak hours.
- Prepare inventory before opening.
- Launch afternoon promotions to improve slower-hour sales.
- Introduce breakfast combo offers during peak demand.

# 🎯 Final Business Recommendations

## 1. Increase Revenue per Customer

Although customer traffic is high, average spending per visit remains relatively low. Focus on upselling larger drink sizes, add-on items, and combo offers.

---

## 2. Protect Best-Selling Products

Top-selling coffee varieties generate a significant share of revenue. Ensure consistent inventory availability and feature them prominently in promotions.

---

## 3. Optimize Morning Operations

Peak demand occurs between **8 AM and 10 AM**. Allocate additional staff and inventory during these hours to improve service speed and customer satisfaction.

---

## 4. Grow Underperforming Categories

Coffee and Tea dominate revenue, while Bakery and Chocolate products have room for growth. Cross-selling and bundled promotions can increase category performance.

---

## 5. Improve Weekend Sales

Weekday sales outperform weekends. Introduce targeted weekend campaigns, seasonal offers, and loyalty rewards to attract more customers.

---

## 6. Improve Data Quality

The large proportion of **"Not Defined"** drink sizes indicates missing information. Standardizing order entry will improve reporting accuracy and support better business decisions.
