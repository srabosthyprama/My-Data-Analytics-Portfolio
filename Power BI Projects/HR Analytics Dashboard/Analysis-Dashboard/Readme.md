# HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built with Power BI to analyze workforce composition, employee performance, compensation, recruitment, satisfaction, hiring trends, and employee turnover.

## Key Insights

### Workforce Overview

- The dataset contains **311 employees**, including **207 active employees** and **104 terminated employees**.
- The overall attrition rate is **33.44%**.
- Average employee satisfaction is **3.89/5**, while average salary is approximately **69K**.
- **Production** is the largest department, with **209 employees** and **83 terminated employees**.

### Recruitment

- **Indeed (87)** and **LinkedIn (76)** are the largest recruitment sources.
- **Google Search** contributed **49 employees**, followed by **Employee Referral** with **31**.
- Recruitment sources can be further evaluated based on employee retention and performance, rather than hiring volume alone.

### Age & Workforce Composition

- The **36–45 age group** has the highest number of active employees (**101**) and also the highest number of attritions (**48**).
- The **46–55** group has **62 active** and **33 attrited** employees, while the **55+** group has **39 active** and **22 attrited** employees.
- These results highlight the importance of understanding employee turnover across different career stages.

### Employee Turnover

- Employee exits peaked during **2015–2016**, with **23 terminations in 2015** and **22 in 2016**.
- The leading termination reasons were **Another Position (20)**, **Unhappy (14)**, **More Money (11)**, and **Career Change (9)**.
- The findings suggest that employee turnover is influenced by multiple factors rather than compensation alone.

### Compensation & Performance

- Employees rated **Exceeds** have the highest average salary at approximately **77K**.
- Employees in **PIP** have the lowest average salary at approximately **59K**.
- Most departments have **Fully Meets** as their dominant performance category.
- The results show a visible relationship between performance levels and average compensation.

### Employee Satisfaction

- Overall average employee satisfaction is **3.89/5**.
- **Unhappiness** is one of the notable recorded termination reasons, highlighting the relevance of employee experience when analyzing employee turnover.

## Key Takeaway

Employee turnover appears to be **multi-dimensional**, with factors such as career opportunities, compensation, satisfaction, and working conditions appearing across the recorded termination reasons.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of workforce patterns across **people, performance, compensation, recruitment, satisfaction, and turnover** to support deeper HR analysis and data-driven decision-making.
